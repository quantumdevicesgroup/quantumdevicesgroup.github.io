<h1 class="visually-hidden">Open positions</h1>

<p class="lead">We are always looking for talented and motivated researchers at all levels to join our team!</p>

<div class="join-grid" markdown="0">
  <div class="section-card">
    <h2>Postdocs</h2>
    <p>Prospective postdocs with experience in experimental quantum science are encouraged to reach out to discuss research opportunities. Please send your CV and motivation to <a href="https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/alp.html">Alp Sipahigil</a>. Eligible candidates are encouraged to consider the postdoc fellowships <a href="{{ '/join/postdoc-fellowships/' | relative_url }}">listed here</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="section-card">
    <h2>Graduate students</h2>
    <p>Prospective students are encouraged to apply to <a href="https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/graduate/research-programs/admissions">EECS</a>, <a href="https://physics.berkeley.edu/i-am-a-uc-berkeley/prospective-students/graduate-admissions">Physics</a>, or <a href="http://ast.berkeley.edu">AS&amp;T</a> PhD programs. Please mention your interest in our group in your application. We recruit students every year! </p>
  </div>
  <div class="section-card">
    <h2>Berkeley undergraduates</h2>
    <p>Berkeley undergraduates interested in quantum hardware research can fill out <a href="https://forms.gle/o2SBvhzBPCZg4sZr6">this research interest form</a>. We have limited spots (about 1–2 per year) to mentor student projects. </p>
    <p>Background in linear circuits (e.g. EE105), electromagnetics (EE117, Physics 110A/B), and quantum mechanics (Physics 137A/B) is preferred. Students interested in superconducting qubit research are strongly encouraged to study course materials <a href="https://qudev.notion.site/ee290">here</a>. If you self-study these materials and solve the assignments, we can likely find a research project for you!</p>
  </div>
</div>
