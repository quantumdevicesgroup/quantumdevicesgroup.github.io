<h1>Privacy statement</h1>

This website of the Quantum Devices Group (Sipahigil Lab) at UC Berkeley does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it has no forms or comments. We do not collect personal information through the site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of visitors for security purposes, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Some pages also load content from other services, which receive your IP address and browser information when they do:

- **Adobe Fonts** supplies the site's typefaces ([Adobe Fonts privacy](https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policies/adobe-fonts.html)).
- **Google Maps** shows our lab locations on the [Facilities](/facilities/) page ([Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **YouTube** plays videos in some news posts, using its privacy-enhanced mode.

Links to other sites, such as journals, Google Scholar and campus pages, are governed by those sites' own policies.

For general information about privacy at UC Berkeley, see the [UC Berkeley Privacy Office](https://privacy.berkeley.edu/). Questions about this site can be sent to [alp@berkeley.edu](mailto:alp@berkeley.edu).
