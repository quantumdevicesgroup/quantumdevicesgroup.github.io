*New NSF collaboration will develop hybrid quantum network architectures by interconnecting superconducting qubits and color centers in silicon*

Like their classical counterparts, quantum technologies will need interconnects that can transduce signals between processors, memories, and communication modules. In this collaboration between UC Berkeley, Caltech, and Virginia Tech, we will develop techniques to interconnect leading solid-state quantum technologies based on superconducting quantum processors and color center quantum memories.

Background on quantum interconnects:

[D. Awschalom et al., "Development of Quantum Interconnects (QuICs) for Next-Generation Information Technologies"](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/PRXQuantum.2.017002)
