Our microwave and optics labs had two big upgrades!

**Optical dilution fridge:**

Our third dilution fridge is online. Hanbin designed this beautiful setup with free-space optical access and widefield imaging capabilities:<br>
Home-built vector magnet, 6-axis piezo positioners for cryogenic photonic probing. And the base temperature is still ~25 mK!

**E-beam evaporator for Josephson junction fabrication:**

This tool is allowing us to more rapidly explore new Josephson junction recipes and processes for novel superconducting devices.
