  - title: "Quantum optics with superconducting circuits"

    text: "At Caltech, superconducting qubits served as artificial atoms in engineered microwave waveguides and metamaterials. This work showed that qubits coupled to a waveguide can act as atom-like mirrors that form a cavity, and explored the dynamics of qubits coupled to structured photonic reservoirs and to a topological waveguide."
  - title: "Quantum transduction"
    thrust: quantum-transduction
    text: "Also at Caltech, a piezo-optomechanical transducer converted the excitation of a superconducting qubit into an optical photon by way of a nanomechanical resonator, and related work realized nano-acoustic resonators with ultralong phonon lifetimes. Related studies explored ytterbium atoms as telecom-band quantum interfaces, coupled to silicon nanophotonics and as a medium for microwave-to-optical conversion."
  - title: "Diamond color centers"
    thrust: past-research
    text: "At Harvard, research on color centers in diamond progressed from quantum interference of photons from remote nitrogen-vacancy centers to indistinguishable photons from separate silicon-vacancy centers. Integrating silicon-vacancy centers with diamond nanophotonics led to a platform for quantum optical networks and to photon-mediated interactions between emitters in a nanocavity, alongside studies of how strain and phonons set their spin coherence."

Past research on topics ranging from diamond color centers to quantum optics and waveguide QED with superconducting circuits and optomechanical quantum transduction. This work, from Alp Sipahigil's PhD at Harvard with Mikhail Lukin and postdoctoral research at Caltech with Oskar Painter, laid the foundations for the group's current research directions.
