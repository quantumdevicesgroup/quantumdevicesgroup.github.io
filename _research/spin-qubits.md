Silicon hosts optically active defects, artificial atoms that emit in the telecom O-band and can be created with CMOS-compatible processing in silicon photonics. The T center combines this telecom optical interface with electron and nuclear spin memories. We engineer these emitters, integrate them into silicon photonic circuits, and work toward quantum network nodes that distribute entanglement over optical fiber.
